결제할 때: Google Play 주문 번호, 결제 토큰, 상품, 결제 · 환불 시각. 카드 번호 등 결제 수단 정보는 받지 않습니다(Google Play가 처리합니다).

광고를 볼 때: 광고 보상 지급을 확인하기 위한 값. 광고는 Google AdMob이 제공하며, AdMob은 광고 ID · 기기 정보 · IP 주소(와 이로 추정한 대략적인 위치) 등을 Google 개인정보처리방침에 따라 수집할 수 있습니다.

계정을 삭제하며 환불을 요청할 때 · 고객 문의를 보낼 때: 이메일 주소와 문의 내용.

다른 이용자에게 보이는 정보: 닉네임, 계정 레벨, 지원 영웅, 마지막 접속 시각, 순위.

3. 보관 기간과 파기

계정을 삭제하면 계정 · Google 로그인 정보 · 게임 진행 · 닉네임 · 친구 정보를 즉시 삭제합니다.

다만 법령과 분쟁 대응을 위해 다음 기록은 이름 · 이메일 없이 가명 식별자로만 남겨 두었다가 기간이 지나면 파기합니다: 결제 기록 5년, 재화 증감 · 소환 기록 3년, 부정 이용 기록 1년, 접속한 날짜 2년, 전투 기록 30일(조작 기록은 7일).

환불 요청에 남긴 이메일은 환불 처리가 끝나면 삭제합니다.

파기는 서버의 기록을 복구할 수 없는 방법으로 지웁니다.

Privacy Policy

1. Information we collect

On first launch (automatically): a guest account identifier, player ID, friend code, a payment-verification identifier (an encrypted value derived from the account identifier), your device language and time zone setting, and the app version.

When you sign in with or link a Google account (optional): your Google account identifier, email address and name (the name on your Google profile). We do not receive these if you do not use Google sign-in.

While you play: nickname, game progress, owned heroes, in-game currency and its change history, battle records (input logs used to verify results), summon history, friend / gift / report / block records, and the dates you played.

When you make a purchase: Google Play order number, purchase token, product, and purchase / refund time. We never receive card numbers or other payment details (Google Play handles them).

When you watch an ad: a value used to confirm the ad reward. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect the advertising ID, device information, IP address (and an approximate location estimated from it), etc. under Google's Privacy Policy.

When you request a refund while deleting your account, or contact support: your email address and message.

Visible to other players: nickname, account level, support hero, last seen time, and ranking.

2. How we use it

To provide the game (saving progress, friends and leaderboard), link accounts (so you can continue on a new device), deliver paid items, prevent duplicate delivery and process refunds, prevent abuse (verifying battle results, blocking abnormal requests), grant ad rewards, answer support requests, and improve the service through anonymous statistics.

3. Retention and deletion

When you delete your account, your account, Google sign-in information, game progress, nickname and friend data are deleted immediately.

To comply with law and handle disputes, the following records are kept only under a pseudonymous identifier (no name or email) and destroyed afterwards: purchase records 5 years, currency and summon history 3 years, abuse records 1 year, play dates 2 years, battle records 30 days (input logs 7 days).

An email address left for a refund request is deleted once the refund is processed.

Records are destroyed by deleting them from our servers in a way that cannot be recovered.

4. Processors and international transfer

We use the following service providers to operate the service.

Supabase, Inc. (USA) — servers, database and authentication. Data is stored on servers in the Republic of Korea (Seoul).

Google LLC (USA) — Google sign-in, Google Play billing and Google AdMob ads.

If you use the service outside the Republic of Korea, the information above is transferred to and processed on servers in Seoul, Republic of Korea.

We do not sell personal information and do not provide it to third parties except as required by law.

5. Device permissions

Internet: to communicate with our servers.

Notifications (optional): key refills, attendance rewards and comeback reminders shown on this device. You can turn them off at any time in the game settings or device settings.

Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob for ads. You can reset or delete it in your device settings.

6. Automatic collection

The app does not use cookies. Your sign-in state and game settings (sound, language, notifications, etc.) are stored only on this device and are removed when you uninstall the app.

7. Security measures

All communication with our servers is encrypted (HTTPS).

Database access is restricted so that each player can read only their own data, and operator access is limited to what operating the service requires.

We neither receive nor store payment method details.

8. Your rights

You may request access to, correction or deletion of your personal information, or ask us to stop processing it. We handle requests within 10 days of receiving them.

Account deletion: delete your account yourself in the game under Settings → Delete account. If you cannot use the app, you can request deletion on the web.

Web deletion request: https://momsmom14.github.io/count-cat/delete-account.html

You can request your play history (currency and summon history) via the contact below.

If you have unused paid Crystals when deleting your account, you can request a refund.

9. Children

This service is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14, and if we learn that a child's personal information was collected without parental consent, we delete it without delay.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will announce it in the game and on this page at least 7 days before it takes effect, or at least 30 days before for changes that significantly affect your rights.
